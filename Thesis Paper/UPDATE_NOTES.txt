Updated IUT BSc Thesis package

This package updates the original IUT thesis source using:
1. The provided IEEE VTC 2027 Spring manuscript and its four updated result figures.
2. The previously audited Multiagent-ReLES-OTA GitHub implementation.
3. The original IUT BSc thesis template files, preserved without modification.

Major updates:
- Reframed the thesis around the updated coupled-channel research question.
- Added the 2x3 architecture-by-conditioning benchmark (IPPO/MAPPO/FP3O x blind/type-conditioned).
- Added the updated coupled-channel return, interference, shield-rate, and payload figures.
- Retained selected earlier thesis figures as explicitly labelled historical isolated-channel diagnostics.
- Added the long-horizon 2,000,897-step validation and its reward-saturation interpretation.
- Corrected the methodological treatment of agent_id vs semantic ecu_type conditioning.
- Added a more complete literature review and refreshed the bibliography with validated metadata/DOIs where available.
- Clarified that the implemented safety filter is CBF-inspired rather than a formal CBF certificate.
- Added a reproducibility appendix and repository-reconciliation notes.

The original IUT class/style files are unchanged.
